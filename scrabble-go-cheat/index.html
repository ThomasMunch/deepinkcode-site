Scrabble GO Cheat

Your shortcut to the best words in Scrabble GO.

Currently available for Android only.

Scrabble GO Cheat scans your Scrabble GO board with OCR, analyzes it with updated dictionaries and a powerful solving algorithm, and shows the best possible words ranked by score, with clear placement on the board. English and French dictionaries.

Screenshots

How it works

  1. 1
    Take a screenshotTake a screenshot of your board in Scrabble GO.
  2. 2
    Import the screenshotOpen Scrabble GO Cheat. Your latest screenshot is imported automatically, or tap “Import” to choose one.
  3. 3
    Pick your wordBrowse the ranked solutions and see where to place each word.

Features

  • Reads the board automatically from a screenshot (OCR)
  • Solutions ranked by points, with exact placement
  • Up-to-date dictionaries
  • No ads
  • Free to try, with Pro Lifetime and Pro Subscription upgrades
