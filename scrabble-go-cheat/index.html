Scrabble GO Cheat

Your shortcut to the best words in Scrabble GO.

Currently available for Android only.

Scrabble GO Cheat scans your Scrabble GO board with OCR, analyzes it with updated dictionaries and a powerful solving algorithm, and shows the best possible words ranked by score, with clear placement on the board. English and French dictionaries.

Use it as a Scrabble GO helper and word finder: no typing in letters, just a screenshot. You get the best Scrabble GO words for your exact board and rack, sorted by score.

    Take a screenshotTake a screenshot of your board in Scrabble GO.
  2. 2
    Import the screenshotOpen Scrabble GO Cheat. Your latest screenshot is imported automatically, or tap “Import” to choose one.
  3. 3
    Pick your wordBrowse the ranked solutions and see where to place each word.

Features

  • Reads the board automatically from a screenshot (OCR)
  • Solutions ranked by points, with exact placement
  • Shows how many tiles are left in the game in total – in the bag plus on your opponent’s rack
  • Up-to-date dictionaries
  • No ads
  • Free to try, with Pro Lifetime and Pro Subscription upgrades

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to type in my letters?

No. Take a screenshot in Scrabble GO and the app reads both the board and your rack automatically.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Scrabble GO Cheat is currently available for Android on Google Play.

Which dictionaries are supported?

English and French.

Is it free?

It is free to download and try. Upgrade to Pro Lifetime – one payment, no subscription – or to Pro Subscription.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Screenshots are only used to read the board and are never sent anywhere. The app can delete them for you afterwards.
