Wordfeud Solver

Ever find yourself stuck on a challenging Wordfeud board, searching for the perfect word to play? Wordfeud Solver reads your board from a screenshot and shows you the best moves, ranked by points.

GET IT ONGoogle PlayDownload on theApp Store

How it works

  1. 1. Take a screenshotTake a screenshot in the Wordfeud app. Make sure not to zoom in on the board.
  2. 2. Import the screenshotOpen Wordfeud Solver. Your latest screenshot is imported automatically. If not, tap “Import” and choose the screenshot.
  3. 3. Pick your moveBrowse the ranked solutions to see their points and where to place them on the board.

Features

  • Reads the board automatically from a screenshot (OCR)
  • Solutions ranked by points, with exact placement
  • Up-to-date dictionaries
  • No ads
  • Free to try, with Pro Lifetime and Pro Subscription upgrades
