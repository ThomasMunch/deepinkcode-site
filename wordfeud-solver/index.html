Wordfeud Solver

Find the highest-scoring Wordfeud move from a single screenshot.

Ever find yourself stuck on a challenging Wordfeud board, searching for the perfect word to play? Wordfeud Solver reads your board from a screenshot and shows you the best moves, ranked by points.

Screenshots

See it in action

The video is played from YouTube when you press play.

How it works

  1. 1
    Take a screenshotTake a screenshot in the Wordfeud app. Make sure not to zoom in on the board.
  2. 2
    Import the screenshotOpen Wordfeud Solver. Your latest screenshot is imported automatically. If not, tap “Import” and choose the screenshot.
  3. 3
    Pick your moveBrowse the ranked solutions to see their points and where to place them on the board.

Features

  • Reads the board automatically from a screenshot (OCR)
  • Solutions ranked by points, with exact placement
  • Shows how many tiles are left in the game in total – in the bag plus on your opponent’s rack
  • Up-to-date dictionaries
  • No ads
  • Free to try, with Pro Lifetime and Pro Subscription upgrades
