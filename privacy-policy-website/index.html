Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy covers the website deepinkcode.com and the apps Wordfeud Solver, Words With Friends Solver (“Words Solver Friend” on the App Store) and Scrabble GO Cheat.

Who we are

DeepInkCode, CVR 45115666, Denmark, is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: support@deepinkcode.com.

The apps

The apps do not require an account and have no server or backend. We do not collect, store or sell personal data.

  • Screenshots: The app reads the screenshot you import in order to recognize the game board and letters. This happens entirely on your device. The image is never uploaded or sent anywhere. In the app’s settings you can choose to have the screenshot deleted after it has been read.
  • Purchases: In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive your payment details or other personal information about you.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking: The apps contain no advertising, no analytics tools and no third-party tracking.

The website

The website uses no cookies and no analytics. Some app pages show a video from YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play; after that, YouTube’s privacy policy applies (we use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode). The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and operations. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Write to support@deepinkcode.com. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

We will update this page if the policy changes.
