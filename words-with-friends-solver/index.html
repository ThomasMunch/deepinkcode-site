Words With Friends Solver

Words With Friends Solver scans your game board with OCR, analyzes it with an up-to-date dictionary and shows the highest-scoring words with clear instructions on where to place them. Supports English, French and Spanish dictionaries.

GET IT ONGoogle PlayDownload on theApp Store

On the App Store the app is called “Words Solver Friend”.

How it works

  1. 1. Take a screenshotTake a screenshot of your board in Words With Friends. Turning off animated tiles gives the best recognition.
  2. 2. Import the screenshotOpen the solver. Your latest screenshot is imported automatically, or tap “Import” to choose one.
  3. 3. Play the best wordSee the solutions ranked by points and exactly where each word goes.

Features

  • Reads the board automatically from a screenshot (OCR)
  • Solutions ranked by points, with exact placement
  • Up-to-date dictionaries
  • No ads
  • Free to try, with Pro Lifetime and Pro Subscription upgrades

Words With Friends Solver is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of Words With Friends.
