Words With Friends Solver

Scan your Words With Friends board and get the best words, ranked by score.

On the App Store the app is called “Words Solver Friend”.

Words With Friends Solver scans your game board with OCR, analyzes it with an up-to-date dictionary and shows the highest-scoring words with clear instructions on where to place them. Supports English, French and Spanish dictionaries.

It works as a Words With Friends cheat, WWF helper and word finder in one: no typing in your letters, just a screenshot. You get the best words for your exact board and rack, sorted by score.

How it works

  1. 1
    Take a screenshotTake a screenshot of your board in Words With Friends. Turning off animated tiles gives the best recognition.
  2. 2
    Import the screenshotOpen the solver. Your latest screenshot is imported automatically, or tap “Import” to choose one.
  3. 3
    Play the best wordSee the solutions ranked by points and exactly where each word goes.

Features

  • Reads the board automatically from a screenshot (OCR)
  • Solutions ranked by points, with exact placement
  • Shows how many tiles are left in the game in total – in the bag plus on your opponent’s rack
  • Up-to-date dictionaries
  • No ads
  • Free to try, with Pro Lifetime and Pro Subscription upgrades

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to type in my letters?

No. Take a screenshot in Words With Friends and the solver reads both the board and your rack automatically.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It is on Google Play as Words With Friends Solver and on the App Store as “Words Solver Friend”.

Which dictionaries are supported?

English, French and Spanish.

Is it free?

It is free to download and try. Upgrade to Pro Lifetime – one payment, no subscription – or to Pro Subscription.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Screenshots are only used to read the board and are never sent anywhere. The app can delete them for you afterwards.
